About

If you’ve lost a beloved pet, this space is for you.

What this project is

This project, Woven into Light, is an interactive website designed to hold and transform expressions of grief. Users are invited to write a message to a pet they have lost, and through a gentle interaction, their words become a floating presence within a shared field of light. Rather than asking users to “let go,” the project focuses on allowing memory and emotion to remain, softly held within the space.

Why it matters

This project is important because the emotional bond between humans and their pets is often as deep as that of family, yet the grief that follows their loss is frequently minimized or overlooked. Many people are left without a socially recognized way to process this kind of attachment and loss.

Who it is for

The website is created for individuals grieving a pet, offering a quiet, non-judgmental environment where their emotions can be expressed and witnessed. By removing metrics such as likes or visibility, the project resists comparison and instead creates a space of care. In doing so, it responds to issues of emotional invisibility and challenges the social hierarchy that determines which forms of grief are considered valid.
